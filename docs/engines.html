Connect

This platform is a switchboard on the internet. It owns no GPU, runs no model and stores no footage. Everything connects to it through one of four plugs, and the creator's own machine or the creator's own key does the work. Nothing is installed on the operator's laptop, and no render is ever billed to the platform by default.

Below the switchboard is the engine road: describe a shot and it ranks every engine that could take it, printing the gate that refused each one it cannot. The switchboard says what is plugged in; the road says which engine to use. Both run src/lib/apex in your browser, so if this page and the repository ever disagree, the page is wrong.

1 · The four plugs

Every engine reaches this platform through exactly one of these. There is no fifth path. The cross-origin column is the browser's rule, not a platform policy: a page may only call an endpoint that permits it, and no amount of frontend code removes that.

2 · The ten stages, and what is behind each

Pick what you have plugged in. The pipeline resolves each stage to a real route or reports not connected with the one thing you would have to bring. A stage is never shown as runnable because the code for it exists.

Separately from the four plugs, the hosted app runs script and storyboard for free on a daily-capped server allowance: DeepSeek V3 with Qwen3 as the open-weights fallback, frames by FLUX schnell, all on Replicate (src/lib/preproduction.functions.ts). That lane lives on the app's server and spends the house's capped allowance, so it is not a plug this static page can offer — video render there stays behind the owner keypad.

3 · Every connector, its plug and its exit

No connector enters this platform without a named exit — if a provider vanishes or reprices, the row already says what runs instead. A licence nobody has read is printed as unverified, never as cleared.

6 · Refused, and why

A shortlist without its refusals is advertising. These engines were met and turned away, and the gate that stopped each one is printed in its own words.

7 · The escape route

Your hardware is never the ceiling of this platform. When the ask is bigger than the body you have, the road names the body that fits and the one thing you must bring. The vendor bills your card; we quote, you decide, and nothing runs until you say so.

8 · The dictionary

Everything the index knows for this stage, measured on the date printed under it. A blank is an honest blank: it means nobody on this platform has verified that number, and an engine with no independent measurement ranks behind one with a measurement — it does not get the benefit of a vendor's claim. Prices move without notice; the citation is the row's last act of honesty.
