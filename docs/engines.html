The Engine Road

We do not own the models and we do not own the compute. We own the road between a creator's idea and the tool that can actually shot it. Describe the shot below. The ranking is not a brochure: every engine that cannot take the job is printed with the gate that refused it — its licence, your territory, the GPU you have, the price, or the fact that it no longer exists. And when nothing on your machine can do it, the road names the body that can and what it costs on your own key.

4 · The escape route

Your hardware is never the ceiling of this platform. When the ask is bigger than the body you have, the road names the body that fits and the one thing you must bring. The vendor bills your card; we quote, you decide, and nothing runs until you say so.
