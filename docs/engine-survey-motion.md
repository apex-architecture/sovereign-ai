# Engine survey — MOTION cluster: stage `motion` (text-to-video and image-to-video — the step that turns a storyboard frame into a moving shot). Compiled 2026-11-04 by human read of primary sources only (Hugging Face model card and raw `LICENSE`, GitHub `LICENSE`/README, vendor API and pricing docs), plus the Artificial Analysis video arena for blind-vote ranks. 27 rows: 18 open-weights, 9 closed APIs. `priceUsdPerSecond` is **USD per finished second of video** on every row here; self-hosted open weights are `0` and the `fits`/`priceSource` say where the money actually goes (the GPU hour). `ceiling.maxSeconds` is the longest *single-pass* generation, never a stitched total.

> Companion to `docs/engine-survey-storyboard.md` and supersedes the twelve `motion` rows currently carried in the dictionary. Two facts reorganise this stage relative to a 2025 read: (1) the open-weight frontier now ships **audio in the same pass** (LTX-2, MiniMax H3, MAGI-2) and **15–30 second takes** (H3 4–15 s, Wan 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 30 s); (2) the best open-weight video model in the world, MiniMax H3 at 33B, is **unlicensed in the USA, EU, UK and Korea**, so rank and legality now point in opposite directions. Where a vendor console is JavaScript-rendered (Kling, Vidu, MiniMax, Google) the price is the independent board's per-second column, labelled as such in `priceSource`; nothing is inferred from a model name.
