## 3. The $0-survival rule

**The rule:** the platform must run at zero cash outflow before it earns a dollar, and it must keep working at zero after it earns. Cash is acceleration, never oxygen (`project-apex-vision-corpus-116-lines.md:10`, spine group: "$0 survival rule; funding is acceleration never oxygen").

**Why this is strategy and not thrift.** The estate's realised paid revenue is zero, stated in its own ledger twice: `/transactions` now prints "Realised revenue to date is zero" (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:127`) and the VC deck "stated realised paid revenue as zero" (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:139`). A platform that needs money to breathe would need a decision from someone else before it could exist. A platform that breathes at $0 needs nothing from anyone, which is the precondition for the one-shot-silence doctrine to be survivable rather than martyrdom (§4 refusal 7).

- `modules.ts:327` — Marketplace is the single row whose status is `gated`: "built only once real supply and demand exist". The architecture refuses to build a mall before there are shops.

1. **It is the only arm where the whole loop already ran at $0.** Three times: the originated 30-second film (§72), the generated-footage tier on local weights (§73), the local finishing run with measured loudness and true peak (§75). No other arm of the estate can show an end-to-end artefact produced for zero. An arm that has already run proves the architecture; an arm that has only been speced proves the ambition.
2. **It has the heaviest real media on the machine.** 424 R3D clips, 62 sync WAVs, 667.11 GiB measured this session (§5 G5). Provenance is easy on a text file and hard on a camera original. If the receipt resolves for a R3D reel, it resolves for everything smaller. That is the thesis in `APEX-ABSOLUTE-DOMINION.md:114` and `_GRAND_MANIFESTO.md:105`: "If you can prove provenance on 667GB of RED raw, you can prove it on anything."
3. **The legal reason to prove it landed on a date, not a vibe.** Article 50 transparency obligations are in force since 2 Aug 2026 with grace to Dec 2026 (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:66`), and the corpus records it as verified fact plus the standing rule that **C2PA is the language to be compatible with, not compete with** (`project-apex-vision-corpus-116-lines.md:21`). A creator shipping AI-assisted work inside that window has a dated duty and no cheap instrument. The wedge is the instrument.
4. **The passion constraint is a durability input, not a soft factor.** Filmmaking is his #1 and he is explicitly tired of the corporate/protocol world (`project-film-platform-first-engine-p-parked.md:10`). A wedge the owner will not abandon is structurally better than a wedge with a bigger theoretical market and no operator attention. The estate's own doctrine already names the failure mode: the model's only fatal surface is that it exists privately, one head, one key, drafts unshipped (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:58`, §4 ONE FIX).
5. **Its channel costs $0 to open and is already mapped.** The arm table puts Film Forge / YouTube as "provenance-sealed media service + brand/cinematic commissions + channel ad revenue", model "service + ads", **cost to open $0**, status "**Dormant till demand**" (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:590`, §22). Dormant is honest: it means the door exists and no customer has walked in. It is not a claim of traction.
6. **The volume fantasy is dead, and that is what makes the wedge survivable.** The estate measured the platform risk directly: YouTube's inauthentic-content policy (renamed 15 Jul 2025, clarified 16 Jul 2026) **demonetises templated 1-2 fully-AI videos a day** — "the exact pattern, not an edge case"; Meta's limited-originality rule and Instagram's AI-profile label do the same; the monetisation thresholds are 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours/365 days or 10M Shorts views/90 days, rising from 1 Feb 2027 to 8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views; Shorts cap at 3 minutes (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:2262-2264`, §75). Therefore the human take — one spoken segment per video — is **load-bearing, not decoration**. The wedge is not "flood the internet with AI films". It is "make work that a platform cannot call templated, and be able to prove how it was made". That is a moat statement with a citation attached.

### 6.2 The wedge's own two-law check (`feedback-two-law-filter-asymmetry-and-alignment.md`)

- **Asymmetric:** cost to attempt is $0 (§3), the artefacts compound (every sealed film is a permanent advertisement for the receipt), the downside is capped at time, and it never needs a lie to survive — because the receipt's promise is deliberately small (§4 refusal 1).
- **Aligned:** it gets stronger if a stranger sees it completely. That is the whole design: the stranger gets the verifier, the public key, the manifest and the anchor, and is handed the knife. Compare the estate's own sales doctrine — "hand them the knife" (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:55`) — and its wall architecture: "hand the reader the instrument; never ask for belief" (`EMPIRE_STATE.md:105`, §26(b)).

### 6.3 How the other verticals attach — the mechanism, in four moves

The architecture already refuses vertical forks. Here is the exact coupling, all of it read from files, none of it proposed:

1. **The registry is the seam.** `TechRecord` (`schema.ts:89-123`) has 27 fields and **no vertical field**. A tool is a `category` (56 already enumerated, `schema.ts:12-69`), an `integrationMethod` (9 kinds: `cli`, `python-api`, `http-api`, `node-graph`, `plugin-or-cli`, `web-app`, `desktop-app`, `protocol`, `library` — `schema.ts:76-86`), and a set of licence verdicts. To add music, games, publishing or photography you add **rows**, not tables and not modules. The category list is the proof the enumeration was done once on purpose: `music`, `sound-fx`, `subtitle`, `three-d`, `animation`, `vfx-compositing`, `games-interactive`, `writing-script`, `design-graphics`, `photography`, `publishing-cms`, `asset-library-3d/image/audio/text` are already there (`schema.ts:12-69`).
2. **A vertical is one execution-layer module.** Every creative vertical in `modules.ts` is exactly that shape: `film`, `image`, `audio`, `threeD`, `games`, `writer`, `design`, `photo` — each in `layer: "execution"`, each `engine: "F"`, each with `dependsOn` pointing only at already-declared shared modules (`tools`, `memory`, `ledger`, `cost`, `library`). `film` depends on `["studio","memory","ledger","cost"]`. `audio` depends on `["tools"]`. So the marginal cost of vertical #7 is one record and one workspace, and the dependency graph enforces it — a vertical cannot reach the platform layer, the intelligence layer, or the trust layer directly.
3. **The proof rails are shared and already named for non-film use.** `psi` (signing, receipts, integrity), `ledger` ("production evidence: sources, contributors, versions, approvals, handovers"), `rights` ("permissions, contributor agreements, licence evidence and holds"), `passport` ("portable creator identity and portfolio, separate from any login"), `verify` (public, no-account) — the `trust-portability` layer holds 6 of 44 modules and is entirely vertical-agnostic. A music catalog and a film catalog consume the identical receipt contract, because `ManifestInput` (`psi.ts:22-31`) records `title`, `contentHash`, `createdOn`, `attribution`, `licenceTerms`, `stageConfig` and `toolDeclarations` — that is a provenance statement for any work with stages, and its `toolDeclarations` field is exactly where "which model made which shot / which stem / which render" lives. It is declared by the creator, and the receipt records the declaration — the seal audits the claim, it does not vouch for it (§4 refusal 1).
4. **The exchange is the gravity, and it is gated.** `workflows` → `exchange` ("creators share and sell whole workflows, not prompts", `modules.ts:147-163`) is how vertical #2 arrives pre-populated rather than from scratch. It sits *after* `marketplace`, which is the one module deliberately marked `gated`. The order is a doctrine in data: build the root, let the branches be traded by other people, and do not open the mall before there are shops.

**The one-line test for "did we re-architect?"** If adding a vertical changes a file in `platform` or `trust-portability`, or adds a migration, or adds a permission field to `TechRecord`, the architecture failed and the change is reverted — not debated. G8 in §5 is that test, written as a count.

### 6.4 Build order the spine implies (this is sequencing, not scope — the scope stays the full platform)

1. **G1 first, all 8 foundation modules to `live`.** `core`, `psi`, `ledger`, `passport`, `verify`, `licenseguard`, `registry`, `connect` — today 0 of those 8 are `live` (`modules.ts:444-453`). No film vertical before this, because a film with nowhere to record its stages is a demo.

4. **G4 → G5.** One original $0 piece sealed end to end, then the 667 GiB camera original. Small artefact first as the *smoke test inside the full build*, never as the deliverable (`feedback-scope-is-always-the-full-platform.md:12`).
